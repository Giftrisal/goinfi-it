Everything You Need to Know

How long does it take to design and launch a website?

+
Standard corporate websites are typically designed and launched within 3 to 5 business days. Full e-commerce stores with automated eSewa, Khalti, and card merchant integrations require approximately 6 to 8 days. Mobile applications depend on feature scope but average 15 to 25 business days.

Can I easily update photos, products, and text myself later?

+
Yes, absolutely! We build all websites with a modern, intuitive visual admin panel. You can easily add or edit products, update prices, change phone numbers, and upload photos in just a few clicks without writing a single line of code. We also provide a complimentary walkthrough video tutorial.

How does the payment schedule work? Do I pay everything upfront?

+
No, you do not pay everything upfront. We operate on a milestone basis: a 50% deposit is required to kick off the design and development phase, and the remaining 50% balance is only due after you thoroughly review, test, and approve the finished project before live launch. Payments can be made via Bank Transfer, eSewa, Khalti, or Fonepay QR.

What are the annual hosting and domain renewal fees after year 1?

+
Your first year includes complimentary high-speed NVMe cloud hosting, SSL encryption, and business email. Starting in year two, annual renewal for managed enterprise cloud hosting, daily automated backups, security patching, and server maintenance is just Rs. 4,999 per year.

Do I retain 100% full ownership of my website and domain?

+
Yes, 100%. All source code, media assets, domain registration rights, and database contents are your exclusive property upon project completion. There are no vendor lock-ins or hidden licensing royalties.
